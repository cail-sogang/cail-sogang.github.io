Our paper is accepted to International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction (IJHCI) 🎉.

<br><br>
This paper introduces that generative AI can help intercultural couples visualize hard-to-express culturally rooted emotions, fostering reflection, empathy, and deeper communication.

---
Title: [Unspoken into the Spoken: Visualizing Cultural Nuances in Intercultural Romantic Dyads through Generative AI-driven Art](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/10447318.2026.2727769)
<br>
Authors: Hyerin Im, Sungho Kim, Seungho Baek, <b>Haedong Jeong</b> and Sunok Lee
